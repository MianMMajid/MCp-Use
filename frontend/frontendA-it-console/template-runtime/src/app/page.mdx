## Control Plane

The console is wired to your gateway and admin APIs through Next.js rewrites (`/api/*` and `/admin/api/*`). Use this surface to verify policy authority, auth posture, and runtime dependencies before team rollout.

## Workflow

1. Check runtime prerequisites in `/maple/runtime`.
2. Validate policy decisions and queues in `/maple/policies` and `/maple/hitl`.
3. Review traces and audit payload shape in `/maple/audit`.

## Live Endpoints

- `GET /api/runtime/feature-capabilities`
- `GET /api/firewall/status`
- `GET /admin/api/policies`
- `GET /api/hitl/list`
